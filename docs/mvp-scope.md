# MVP Scope

## Must Have

### Farmer
- registration/login;
- farm profile;
- location;
- weather;
- crop recommendation;
- market insight;
- crop listing.

### Buyer
- marketplace;
- filters;
- listing details;
- order/inquiry.

### Admin
- user verification;
- listing moderation;
- analytics.

## Should Have

- crop comparison;
- explainable recommendation;
- demand heat indicator;
- notifications;
- multilingual UI labels.

## Could Have

- disease image detection;
- voice assistant;
- satellite monitoring;
- logistics optimization;
- contract farming.

## Won't Have in Initial Hackathon MVP

- real payment gateway;
- real logistics settlement;
- production-scale financial services;
- complex satellite ML pipeline.

## Golden Demo

```text
Farmer Login
    ↓
Farm Details
    ↓
Weather + Market Analysis
    ↓
AI Crop Recommendation
    ↓
Create Crop Listing
    ↓
Buyer Marketplace
    ↓
Order Request
    ↓
Admin Approval
    ↓
Analytics
```

If time becomes limited, preserve this flow and cut secondary features.
